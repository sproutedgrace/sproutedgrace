Statement of Faith

We stand on three roots, drawn from the words of Christ.

"I am the way, the truth, and the life"

JOHN 14:6

I. The Truth

The Bible is the Word of the GodFather. The words of Jesus Christ (Yahushua), the red letters, are its heart. We also hold the Gospel of Thomas as a witness to His sayings.

There is one God: the GodFather, the Son and the Holy Spirit. He is the same God in the Old and New Testaments. He has grown from judgment into full grace. We receive His grace, and we give it back to Him.

Jesus was born of a virgin. He gave Himself for our sins, rose from the dead, and will return. We are saved by grace, through faith.

"For the Son of man is not come to destroy men's lives, but to save them"

LUKE 9:56

John 6:63 · Matthew 24:35 · Matthew 28:19 · Luke 9:55-56 · John 3:16 · Why we say GodFather →

II. The Way

We keep the Ten Commandments. We do no harm, and we take nothing that is not ours. Every person bears the image of the GodFather, so we reject genocide.

The GodFather made the body on purpose, even the endocannabinoid system. Plant medicines, received with reverence and within the law, can be holy communion with the Spirit. We hold that kaneh bosem, in the holy anointing oil, was cannabis. We will use that oil only once we are legally exempt.

To be sober-minded is to keep a clear, watchful mind under the GodFather. It does not mean staying away from every plant He made.

We obey the laws of man wherever they do not oppose Scripture or natural law. Where they do, we obey the GodFather rather than men.

"Render therefore unto Caesar the things which are Caesar's; and unto God the things that are God's"

MATTHEW 22:21

Matthew 22:37-40 · Matthew 7:12 · Matthew 10:30 · Mark 7:15 · Thomas 14 · Luke 10:34 · Luke 21:34 · Exodus 30:23 · About plant medicine →

III. The Life

Baptism in the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit brings us into Christ's family. We honor baptism given to a child by faithful family.

The Spirit sends each of us out as an ambassador of Heaven. Fear, guilt, anger, hurt, frustration, disappointment and inadequacy are gates that bind the soul. The grace of Christ sets us free from them. Free in Christ, we walk in wisdom.

"If the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free indeed"

JOHN 8:36

Mark 16:16 · Mark 10:14 · John 20:21 · Matthew 16:18 · Matthew 10:16 · Thomas 5 · Thomas 70

Why We Say GodFather

We worship Yahuah, the I AM, the Most High. We call Him the GodFather. Here is why.

His names

  • Yahuah. The name He gave Moses at the burning bush, written in Hebrew with four letters, YHWH.
  • I AM. He told Moses, "I AM THAT I AM" (Exodus 3:14). Jesus used the same words of Himself.
  • The Most High. Jesus said those who love their enemies will be "the children of the Highest" (Luke 6:35).

"Before Abraham was, I am"

JOHN 8:58

Why "Father"

Jesus called Him Father more than any other name. When He taught us to pray, He began with "Our Father."

"After this manner therefore pray ye: Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name"

MATTHEW 6:9

Why "GodFather"

  • He is both. One word holds His power as God and His love as Father.
  • He claims us. At a baptism, a godfather stands with the child and promises to raise them in faith. The GodFather does this for each of us. He claims us, stands for us, and raises us.
  • He grew into grace. In the Old Testament we see Him in judgment. In Christ we see Him in full grace. When the disciples wanted to call down fire, Jesus stopped them.
  • We give grace back. As He gives grace to us, we give grace to Him and to others.

"Ye know not what manner of spirit ye are of. For the Son of man is not come to destroy men's lives, but to save them"

LUKE 9:55-56

"Freely ye have received, freely give"

MATTHEW 10:8

The Sabbath Rest

The seventh day is a gift of rest. We gather on it, and each person keeps it by conscience.

What it is

The Sabbath is the seventh day. In the Bible, a day runs from evening to evening, so the Sabbath runs from Friday at sunset to Saturday at sunset. Keeping it holy is the fourth of the Ten Commandments (Exodus 20:8-11).

"The sabbath was made for man, and not man for the sabbath: Therefore the Son of man is Lord also of the sabbath"

MARK 2:27-28

How we gather

We meet online every Saturday at 11 AM Central. We worship, hear the Word, and share Communion. Follow @sproutedgraceministry to join us.

How to rest

  • Set down your work.
  • Spend time with the GodFather, in prayer and in His words.
  • Be with family, friends and creation.
  • Do good. Jesus healed on the Sabbath.

"Wherefore it is lawful to do well on the sabbath days"

MATTHEW 12:12

By conscience

The Sabbath was made to serve you, not to weigh you down. Keep it as your heart leads. No one is judged by how they keep it.

The Appointed Feasts

In Leviticus 23, the GodFather set seven holy feasts for His people: Passover, Unleavened Bread, Firstfruits, Weeks, Trumpets, the Day of Atonement and Tabernacles. Jesus kept them, and many point to Him. We honor them, and each family keeps them by conscience.

"With desire I have desired to eat this passover with you before I suffer"

LUKE 22:15

The Clean Table

Deuteronomy 14 lists the foods the GodFather called clean. Some of us keep this way of eating, as care for the body He made.

What it is

  • Land animals: those that chew the cud and have split hooves, like cattle, sheep, goats and deer. Not pigs, camels or rabbits.
  • Water animals: those with fins and scales, like salmon, trout and cod. Not shellfish, catfish or eels.
  • Birds: most common birds, like chicken, turkey and duck. Not birds of prey or scavengers, like eagles, vultures, ravens and owls.
  • No animal that died on its own.

Deuteronomy 14:3-21

Why we keep it

Jesus taught that food does not make a person unclean. What makes us unclean comes from the heart.

"There is nothing from without a man, that entering into him can defile him: but the things which come out of him, those are they that defile the man"

MARK 7:15

So we do not keep the clean table to be made holy or to be saved. Grace does that. We keep it, by choice, as a way to honor and care for the body the GodFather made.

By conscience

Each person chooses for themselves. No one is judged by what is on their plate.
