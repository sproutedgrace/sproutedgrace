About

BLOCK 04

Rooted in Restoration

Sprouted Grace is a private, faith-based education platform devoted to blockchain and legal sovereignty. The goal is simple: help you build on systems that can’t be frozen or seized, and stand in the law without fear.

It’s run by Andrew Strand — an entrepreneur and self-represented (sui juris) litigant who learned this material the hard way, then put it to work. Faith in Yahushua turned his life around, and that same conviction is behind everything taught here. Think less pulpit, more older brother who has already made the mistakes and figured a few things out.

Everything here comes from lived experience — not theory sold from a distance. If it’s taught on this site, it’s because it was walked first.
