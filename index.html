Statement of Faith

What we believe

Our faith grows from five roots.

ROOT I

Christ's Words First

The Bible is God's Word, and the red letters of Jesus are our heart. We follow one God: the GodFather, His Son Yahushua, and the Holy Spirit. We are saved by grace, as a free gift.

John 6:63
ROOT II

God's Law of Love

We keep the Ten Commandments and rest on the Sabbath, Saturday. We do no harm, and we take nothing that is not ours. Every person is made in God's image.

Matthew 22:37-40
ROOT III

Made on Purpose

God made our bodies with care, even the endocannabinoid system. He gave plants for healing. Used with care and within the law, they can be holy. A sober mind is a clear mind under God.

Revelation 22:2
ROOT IV

Born of the Spirit

We are baptized in the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit. The Spirit lives in us and changes us. Each of us is an ambassador of Heaven, sent by Christ to share His message with the world.

2 Corinthians 5:20
ROOT V

Healed and Free

God heals the whole person. We bring hidden hurt into the light. Christ set us free, so we live free and wise.

Galatians 5:1
